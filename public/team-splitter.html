The aim: keep both teams in Gold or Silver (never Platinum) and, ideally, in different divisions so your two teams are never drawn against each other.

Team totals are the sum of the three core players' ratings · caps: Silver 1,500 · Gold 1,650 · Platinum 1,800 (avoided) · subs don't count toward the cap.
Division is set strictly by rating — a core ≤ 1,500 plays Silver, 1,501–1,650 plays Gold (no playing up). Two teams in the same division can be drawn against each other; only a Silver + Gold split (one core under 1,500, one over) keeps them apart. Both-Silver is the cheapest, but the two teams unavoidably share the Silver division.
