In-Depth Brand Profiles

01 Heirbloom The Connoisseur's Pick
Vibe · High-end legacy craft

Cultivated by the multi-award-winning Cannabiotix (CBX) team. Specializes in preserving clean, robust, rare legacy genetics (Green Crack, Master Kush, Durban Poison). Expect room-shaking aromas, maximum trichome coverage, and a pure entourage effect.

02 STIIIZY The Powerhouse
Vibe · Mass-commercial, high-octane potency

California powerhouse focusing on high-potency flower. Famous for their 40s line — premium flower boosted to 40%+ THC by infusing buds with live resin and coating them in pure kief.

03 Henry's Original The Clean Smoke
Vibe · Artisanal and eco-conscious

Grown in small batches across Mendocino County using strict Clean Green Certified organic practices. Avoids harsh chemical inputs, yielding an exceptionally smooth, natural, legacy-style smoke.

04 Glass House Farms The Daily Benchmark
Vibe · High-tech greenhouse quality at a fair price

Climate-controlled greenhouses in Santa Barbara. Sets the standard for price-to-quality ratio. Properly cured, full-sized buds in glass jars — great terpene flavor on popular strains like Super Boof and Lilac Diesel.

05 Pacific Stone The Reliable Standard
Vibe · Simple, honest, farm-direct weed

Parent brand to Time Machine. Completely vertical, single-source family farm in Santa Barbara. Consistent, middle-of-the-road greenhouse flower — clean, functional, reliable.

06 Time Machine The Volume King
Vibe · Bulk party packs and budget daily smoking

Same greenhouse ecosystem as Pacific Stone, focus shifted to bulk convenience. Pioneered value packaging formats like the 28-count, half-ounce pre-roll jar.

07 Cloud The Ultra-Budget Option
Vibe · Practical "working-class" cannabis

Lowest entry price for loose eighths and half-ounces. Lacks complex terpene depth and bag appeal but reliably delivers functional highs on classic strains (Pink Runtz, OG Kush).
