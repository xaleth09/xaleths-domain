The trigger

  • The moment the last card of the Artisan deck or the Enchantment deck is drawn or revealed, the end is triggered.
  • Everyone gets one final turn — including the player who set it off.
❤

Final scoring, in this order

  • Return all your Coins to the Fountain and score 1 ❤ each.
  • Reveal every Moon 🌙 Fancy Dragon whose goal you fulfilled and score it.
Order mattersCoins go back before Fancy Dragons score — so a Coin can never help fulfil a Fancy Dragon’s goal.
TiesMost Artisan Dragons left in hand wins. Still tied? Most leftover Goods. Still tied? Share the victory.
